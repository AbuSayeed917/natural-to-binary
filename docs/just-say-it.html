    How it works: your words go to Claude, which decides what kind of thing you want. Calculations on whole numbers come back as a WebAssembly binary written byte by byte. This page validates it, runs Claude's own test cases, and sends any failure back for repair (up to 3 tries). Everything else comes back as a small app running in a sealed box. The code still exists, it's just not your problem unless you peek.
