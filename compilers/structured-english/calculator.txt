# A calculator, in Structured English v0.1
The screen is charcoal.
A display at the top shows numbers in lime.
Buttons, four per row: 7 8 9 ÷ 4 5 6 × 1 2 3 − C 0 = +.
The operation buttons are orange.
Remember a number called entry, a number called total, and an operation.

When I press a digit:
  Entry becomes entry times 10 plus the digit.
  Show entry.

When I press an operation:
  Total becomes entry.
  Remember the operation.
  Entry becomes 0.

When I press equals:
  Apply the operation to total and entry, and put the answer in entry.
  Show entry.

When I press C:
  Entry becomes 0.
  Total becomes 0.
  Show entry.
