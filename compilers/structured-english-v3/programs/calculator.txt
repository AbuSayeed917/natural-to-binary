# A calculator with decimals
The screen is charcoal.
Write "CALCULATOR" at the top in grey.
A display shows numbers in lime.
Buttons, four per row: 7 8 9 ÷ 4 5 6 × 1 2 3 − C 0 . +.
Buttons, one per row: =.
Remember a number called entry, a number called total, and an operation.

When I press a digit:
  Type the digit into entry.
  Show entry.

When I press .:
  Type a point into entry.
  Show entry.

When I press an operation:
  Total becomes entry.
  Remember the operation.
  Clear entry.

When I press equals:
  Apply the operation to total and entry, and put the answer in entry.
  Show entry.

When I press C:
  Clear entry.
  Total becomes 0.
  Show entry.
