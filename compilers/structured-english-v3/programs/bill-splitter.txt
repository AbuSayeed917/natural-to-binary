# Split a bill between friends
The screen is teal.
Write "SPLIT THE BILL" at the top in white.
Remember a number called bill, and a number called people starting at 2.
A display, labelled "BILL", shows bill as money in white on black.
A display, labelled "PEOPLE", shows people in white on black.
A display, labelled "EACH PAYS", shows bill divided by people as money in yellow on black.
Buttons, two per row: "FEWER" "MORE".
Buttons, four per row: 7 8 9 C 4 5 6 . 1 2 3 0.

When I press a digit:
  Type the digit into bill.
When I press .:
  Type a point into bill.
When I press C:
  Clear bill.
When I press "MORE":
  People becomes people plus 1.
When I press "FEWER":
  If people is more than 1, people becomes people minus 1.
