# A counter with round buttons
The screen is purple.
Write "COUNTER" at the top in white.
Remember a number called count.
A display shows count in white on black.
Buttons, three per row: − "RESET" +.
The buttons are round.
The operation buttons are pink.
The "RESET" button is grey.

When I press +:
  Count becomes count plus 1.
When I press −:
  If count is more than 0, count becomes count minus 1.
When I press "RESET":
  Count becomes 0.
