# A stopwatch
The screen is charcoal.
Write "STOPWATCH" at the top in white.
Remember a number called time, and a number called running.
A display shows time in lime.
Buttons, two per row: "START" "STOP".
Buttons, one per row: "RESET".
The "START" button is green.
The "STOP" button is salmon.

Every tenth of a second:
  If running is 1, time becomes time plus 0.1.
When I press "START":
  Running becomes 1.
When I press "STOP":
  Running becomes 0.
When I press "RESET":
  Running becomes 0.
  Time becomes 0.
