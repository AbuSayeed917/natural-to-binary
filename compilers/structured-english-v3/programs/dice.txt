# A dice roller
The screen is green.
Write "DICE ROLLER" at the top in white.
Remember a number called die starting at 1, a number called total, and a number called rolls.
A display, labelled "YOU ROLLED", shows die in white on black.
A display, labelled "ROLLS", shows rolls in sky on black.
A display, labelled "TOTAL", shows total in yellow on black.
Buttons, one per row: "ROLL".
Buttons, one per row: "START AGAIN".
The "START AGAIN" button is grey.

When I press "ROLL":
  Die becomes a random number from 1 to 6.
  Total becomes total plus die.
  Rolls becomes rolls plus 1.
When I press space:
  Die becomes a random number from 1 to 6.
  Total becomes total plus die.
  Rolls becomes rolls plus 1.
When I press "START AGAIN":
  Total becomes 0.
  Rolls becomes 0.
