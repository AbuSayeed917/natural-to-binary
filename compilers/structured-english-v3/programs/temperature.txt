# Celsius to Fahrenheit
The screen is blue.
Write "°C TO °F" at the top in white.
Remember a number called celsius.
A display, labelled "CELSIUS", shows celsius in white on black.
A display, labelled "FAHRENHEIT", shows celsius times 9 divided by 5 plus 32 in yellow on black.
Buttons, four per row: 7 8 9 C 4 5 6 − 1 2 3 0.
The buttons are sky.
The C buttons are salmon.

When I press a digit:
  Type the digit into celsius.
When I press −:
  Celsius becomes 0 minus celsius.
When I press C:
  Clear celsius.
